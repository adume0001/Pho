The menu

Dishes worth the queue

Four classics you will find on almost every corner. Try them all in one day if you can.

Four classics

Breakfast

Phở

Flat rice noodles in a clear, spiced beef or chicken broth, topped with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lime.

Pair it with: quẩy, crispy fried dough sticks.

Lunch

Bún chả

Smoky grilled pork patties in a sweet and sour broth, served with rice vermicelli and a plate of fresh herbs.

Pair it with: nem rán, fried spring rolls.

Anytime

Bánh mì

A crisp baguette filled with pâté, pickled carrot, cucumber, herbs and your choice of meat.

Tip: ask for it with extra chili if you like heat.

Afternoon

Cà phê trứng

Strong coffee topped with a thick, sweet whipped egg-yolk cream. Think of it as dessert in a cup.

Tip: stir slowly, then sip.
