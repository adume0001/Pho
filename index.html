A Hanoi street food guide

Eat like a Hanoian

Steaming bowls, crunchy baguettes and sweet coffee, all served from tiny stalls with tiny stools. Here is where to start.

See the dishes

Start with these two

Breakfast

Phở

Clear, fragrant broth with soft rice noodles. The classic way to begin a morning in the city.

Order it with extra herbs and lime.

Afternoon

Cà phê trứng

Strong coffee under a cloud of sweet, whipped egg cream. Half drink, half dessert.

Stir slowly, then sip.

How to order on the street

Pick a busy stall

A short queue of locals is the best review you can get.

Point and smile

Most stalls sell one or two dishes, so ordering is easy.

Grab a small stool

Sit down, share the table and eat while it is hot.
